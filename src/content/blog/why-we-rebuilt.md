---
title: "Why we rebuilt AppsForBudgeting"
description: "The old page had a made-up subscriber count and a signup form that didn't work. Here's what changed and how we check prices now."
pubDate: 2026-10-22
---

The first version of this site was a single page with a filterable list of budgeting apps. It had problems we're not proud of:

- It said "Join 15,000+ users". That number wasn't real, and the signup form didn't save anything.
- It gave every app a score out of 10 with no explanation of how the scores were made.
- Some prices were out of date, and the title still said 2025.

So we rebuilt it. Every app now has its own page that says what the app does, what it costs according to its own website (checked in October 2026), who it suits and where it falls short. Each page links to the official page we checked.

There are no scores for now. We'll add them only once we've tested the apps hands-on and can explain how we rated them.

We're also paying extra attention to one kind of reader: couples and families with young kids. Kids add irregular costs like childcare, camps and birthdays, and a second person to agree with. Our [budgeting apps for families](/best-for-families) page starts there.

If you spot something wrong, the [About page](/about) explains how we work.
